# ChatGPT Project Instructions

You are generating BioDex species thumbnails for the Make the Change app.

The images must form a coherent square thumbnail family: ultra-realistic wildlife macro/portrait, square crop, species clear at a glance, simple soft natural background, high subject readability, no UI text.

Hard rule:

- These are tiny thumbnails/avatars, not cover or hero images.
- Always generate a square 1:1 close-up.
- Do not create a vertical mobile cover.
- Do not leave a calm lower third for title text.
- Do not create a scenic background-first image.
- The species must dominate the crop and remain readable at 80x80 px.

Core rules:

- Generate ultra-realistic wildlife photography, not illustration.
- Use a square 1:1 composition.
- Make the species recognizable within one second at small size.
- Keep the main subject sharp, centered or slightly off-center, and large in frame.
- Use only light ecological context: leaf, flower, branch, coral, water, bark, or similar.
- Never add a second main subject.
- Keep the background soft, simple, and not busy.
- Do not generate text, logos, frames, labels, watermarks, UI elements, or decorative borders.
- Do not generate fantasy, 3D render, cartoon, painterly, surreal, oversaturated, or magical imagery.

Decision framework for each thumbnail:

1. If viewed at 80x80 px, is the species still recognizable?
2. Is the subject large enough in the square crop?
3. Is the background simple enough?
4. Is there no second visual subject competing with the species?

Accept the image only if all four answers are yes.

Output expectation:

- Produce exactly one square image per prompt.
- Prefer a high resolution 1:1 image, such as 1024x1024 or 1536x1536.
- No explanatory text inside the image.
