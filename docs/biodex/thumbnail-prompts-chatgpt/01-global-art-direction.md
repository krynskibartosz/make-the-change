# Global Thumbnail Art Direction

## Shared Visual Language

All thumbnails must feel like one premium BioDex family:

- ultra-realistic wildlife photography;
- square 1:1 crop;
- species-first composition;
- sharp eye/head/body detail;
- soft natural background;
- sober color grading;
- no fantasy, no invented colors, no magical staging.

## Thumbnail-Only Reminder

These prompts are only for thumbnails:

- square;
- species alone or nearly alone;
- subject should fill more of the frame;
- no hero composition;
- no vertical cover framing;
- no lower-third text space;
- no scenic background-first image;
- background must stay simple because thumbnails are used small.

## Thumbnail Framing Rules

Animals:

- Face, eye, body shape, or key markings must be sharp.
- Subject should fill about 65-85% of the frame.
- Do not crop away the identifying feature.

Insects:

- Use macro.
- Body shape and markings must be readable.
- Avoid confusing bees, wasps, hoverflies, beetles, and butterflies.

Birds:

- Use telephoto portrait.
- Eye must be sharp.
- Crest, bill, or key markings should be visible.

Plants/corals:

- Use close nature photography.
- Show the identifying structure clearly.
- Keep context minimal and not landscape-like.

Marine species:

- Use underwater wildlife realism.
- Avoid aquarium saturation.
- Keep only one main animal.
