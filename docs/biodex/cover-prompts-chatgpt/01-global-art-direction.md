# Global Art Direction

## Shared Visual Language

All covers must feel like one premium BioDex family:

- ultra-realistic wildlife photography;
- mobile-first portrait framing;
- recognizable main species;
- natural habitat;
- shallow depth of field;
- soft background separation;
- sober color grading;
- calm lower third for UI overlays;
- no fantasy, no invented colors, no magical staging.

## Asset Families

Thumbnail images:

- Square.
- Species alone or nearly alone.
- Highly legible at small sizes.
- Existing files in `apps/web-client/public/images/species-thumbnails/` are considered valid.

Diorama images:

- Do not regenerate.
- Existing UI/illustration assets stay in `apps/web-client/public/images/dioramas/`.

Cover images:

- Vertical mobile-first wildlife covers.
- Stored in `apps/web-client/public/images/biodex/`.
- Filename pattern: `hero-<scientific-name-slug>.png`.

## Biome Variants

Madagascar humid rainforest:

- deep green vegetation;
- humid air;
- filtered canopy light;
- realistic darker forest atmosphere;
- soft bokeh and moss/leaf context.

Belgian and European pollinators:

- natural macro photography;
- warm light;
- meadow, garden, orchard, hedgerow, or wildflower context;
- subject very detached from background.

Sardinia and Mediterranean:

- warm dry golden light;
- olive leaves, scrubland, stone, wildflowers;
- silver-green vegetation;
- premium but not tourist-poster.

Indonesian coral reef:

- realistic underwater wildlife;
- blue filtered natural light;
- clear but not flashy;
- no aquarium look;
- coral/seagrass context must not overpower the species.

Birds:

- telephoto wildlife feel;
- natural perch;
- soft background;
- eye sharpness is critical.

Insects:

- macro or close wildlife photography;
- exact body shape and markings matter;
- no wasp/bee/fly confusion.

Marine species:

- underwater realism;
- moderate saturation;
- natural light beams and water depth;
- no neon aquarium palette.
